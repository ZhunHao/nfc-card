Personal NFC card: shortlist

Each variant reuses the NTUAS electronics unchanged: NTAG I2C Plus energy harvesting, an ATtiny1616 Charlieplexing up to 20 red LEDs on five pins, and the four-turn perimeter antenna. Only the LED positions, routing, silkscreen and mask colour change. Drawn at ID-1 scale (85.60 × 53.98 mm). The simulated animation lights at most two LEDs at a time, as the real firmware must.

Data sources

  • Stars (A): J2000 positions and V magnitudes from the SIMBAD database (CDS Strasbourg) via astroquery 0.4.11. Each field uses a gnomonic projection with north up and east to the left, as seen looking up. No rotation is applied. Where two stars sit closer than an LED footprint (Shaula and Lesath, Atlas and Pleione, β and α Muscae), the fainter one is a printed dot.
  • ISS (C2, C4): TLE from CelesTrak, epoch 24 Oct 2026, propagated with skyfield 1.55. Predictions a week out can drift by a minute or so; re-run before printing a date.
  • Sky at the pass (C4): alt/az of bright stars over NTU (1.3483° N, 103.6831° E) at the pass peak, from SIMBAD positions and astropy 8.0.2.

Fixed by the hardware, same for every variant

  • LEDs stay red. The harvested rail is about 2 V. The NTUAS plan bans blue, white and green LEDs because their forward voltage is too high to light. Mask colour carries each design's identity instead.
  • Up to 20 LEDs, one or two lit at once. Five Charlieplex pins drive at most 20, and the phone supplies about 5 mA.
  • Keep-out band: the antenna occupies 4.0–6.45 mm in from every edge, so all art sits inside about 72 × 41 mm.
  • Parts on the front only (single-sided assembly), so the MCU block appears on every front. The back is free for contact text.
  • JLCPCB mask colours: green, purple, red, yellow, blue, white, black (JLCPCB capabilities).
