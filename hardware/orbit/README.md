# C1 Orbit to scale

Earth's limb and two orbits at true scale on an ID-1 card: Earth R = 80 mm centred at
concept (43, 118), so 1 mm ≈ 79.64 km. ISS orbit r = 85.274 mm (420 km), upper orbit
r = 105.114 mm (2,000 km, top of LEO). Every arc, bus and LED is concentric with Earth.
Concept coordinates are mm from the card's top-left; KiCad = concept + 100.

Rev B electronics: the
NTUAS circuit with U2 as ATtiny1616-MNR (VQFN-20 3 × 3 mm), a UPDI ESD diode D21, a soft-start
reservoir (Q1, R7, R8, C4), test pads on the back and antenna option A. U1, C1, C2 and the U1
end of the LA/LB routing are the NTUAS card's; the LA feed to the coil is rebuilt (see Routing).
Everything else on the board is generated by `build_orbit.py` (KiCad 10 Python, idempotent):

```bash
/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3 hardware/orbit/build_orbit.py
```

## LED mapping

LED index k is D[k+1]: anode CP[k/4], cathode CP[c], c = (k%4) < (k/4) ? k%4 : k%4+1.
Positions are listed left to right. `firmware/boards/orbit/animation.c` uses the same order.

| Row | x (concept mm) | Ref (index k) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISS, r 85.274 | 12, 19.8, 27.6, 35.4, 43.2, 51, 58.8, 66.6, 74.4 | D2 (1), D3 (2), D1 (0), D4 (3), D6 (5), D7 (6), D8 (7), D5 (4), D9 (8) |
| 2,000 km, r 105.114 | 11, 19.5, 28, 36.5, 45, 53.5, 62, 70.5 | D10 (9), D13 (12), D14 (13), D15 (14), D17 (16), D18 (17), D19 (18), D20 (19) |
| Ground stations, limb + 2.6 | 27.6, 43, 66 | D11 (10), D16 (15), D12 (11) |

The station LEDs sit under the ISS LEDs at x = 27.6, 43.2 and 66.6, which light them in the animation.

## Deviations from the concept

- **Ground station x = 20 moved to x = 27.6** (still on the limb, 2.6 mm inside it). At x = 20 it
  landed on U1/C2 at the antenna feed. 27.6 puts it under an ISS LED like the other two.
- All LEDs are rotated tangent to their orbit (concept draws them axis-aligned).
- Labels are raised from 0.95 to 1.0 mm (JLCPCB standard minimum, 0.15 mm stroke) in KiCad's
  stroke font, not JetBrains Mono. Letter spacing is not available except for `E A R T H`,
  which is set letter by letter. `ISS · 420 km` starts at x = 43.5 (concept 47) to stay off the
  routing trunk; `2,000 km · TOP OF LEO` sits 2.4 mm above its arc (concept 1.4) because the
  wider stroke font would otherwise cover the LEDs at x = 36.5 and 45.
- **LED cathode dots** (after order Y8): a 0.4 mm silk dot beside each LED's pad 1 (the
  cathode), on the side away from its bus stubs (outward on the ISS row, inward on the
  2,000 km row and the stations), 0.27 mm clear of the pad. The library LED outline sits
  0.1 mm from the pads, so the 0.25 mm silk cut-back removed it and Y8 shipped with no LED
  polarity mark, which led JLC to ask for a polarity check. A dot past the pad end would land
  on the orbit line and look like an orbit dot. The station chevrons are 0.35 mm lower than
  the concept (tip 1.2 mm below the LED centre) to clear the dots.
- Silk lines are all ≥ 0.15 mm. The concept's faint 0.09 mm rings and meridians are drawn
  dashed at 0.15 mm instead of at reduced opacity. Silk stops 0.25 mm short of pads.
- MCU block (concept mm): U2 (VQFN-20) at (69.5, 23.0), 90°, pin 1 bottom-left. R1–R5 stay in a
  column at x = 61.8 by the trunk (y = 23.8 … 27.8, PA1 … PA5). C3 sits under U2's VCC/GND pins at
  (69.7, 26.7), JP1 at (61.2, 30.6), R6 above U2 at (66.7, 20.6). The soft-start sits right of U2:
  R8 (74.2, 21.3), R7 (74.2, 22.3), Q1 (73.9, 23.6), C4 (72.75, 22.6). `LIFT JP1 TO FLASH` from
  the NTUAS card is dropped; JP1 is labelled.
- **Test pads on the back** (rev B): TP1/TP2/TP3 (**UPDI / VCC / GND**, VCC in the middle) are
  1.5 mm pads on B.Cu in a vertical column at 2.54 mm pitch, x = 77.5, y = 7.8 / 10.34 / 12.88,
  for a 0.1" pogo clip. Seen from the back they sit top-left, above the name, with mirrored
  B.SilkS labels (1.0 mm tall, 0.8 mm glyphs) aligned with the contact block. Each pad is fed by
  a via 1.25 mm to its left, never in the pad. The front TP labels are gone.
- D21 (UPDI TVS) sits on the front directly behind TP1, at (77.55, 8.25); its GND pad runs down
  to TP3's via.
- Back: `TAP TO CONNECT` and its glyph moved 2 mm toward the centre to clear the antenna band.
- Back: the two TLE lines keep the 1.0 mm height but use 0.62 mm-wide glyphs, so they run
  about 39 mm like the concept's monospace (37.6 mm) instead of 62 mm. JLC's line gap is still met.

## Routing

Each orbit row has five concentric B.Cu bus arcs, one per Charlieplex net (ISS buses inside the
ISS row, 2,000 km buses outside its row, pitch 0.85 mm). Every LED pad drops a short radial F.Cu
stub to a via on its net's bus. One radial F.Cu trunk (through the ISS orbit at x = 55, between
LEDs) joins both bus sets; B.Cu branches tap it to R1–R5. The stations share the ISS buses: the
ISS LED above each station only uses buses outside both of the station's, so their stubs never
meet. VOUT and GND leave U1/C2 on B.Cu arcs inside Earth (r ≈ 78 and 76.5 mm) and ride the trunk
up to JP1 and the MCU. No autorouter was used; DRC (error severity) is clean with no unconnected
items and no parity issues.

**MCU block fan-out (VQFN-20, 0.4 mm pitch).** Escapes are 0.15 mm straight out of each pad.
PA1 (pin 20, left side) and PA2/PA3 (pins 1/2, bottom side) run to R1–R3 on F.Cu as nested
L-shapes. PA4/PA5 (pins 5/6) drop to vias beside the package and pass under the block on B.Cu to
vias next to R4/R5. GND (pin 3) is tied to the exposed pad by a short track inside the body
(no ground plane on this card) and, with VCC (pin 4), runs straight down into C3. UPDI (pin 19)
goes up through R6 and over the top of U2; SS_GATE (pin 15) runs alongside to R8 and Q1's gate.
VCC goes JP1 → C3 → a spine up the right side to TP2, with the soft-start parts and Q1's source
stubbed onto it. GND runs on B.Cu directly under that VCC rail and spine (a broadside pair, so
the VCC/GND loop closed by C3 and C4 encloses almost nothing) up to TP3, with a branch to C4.

**Antenna feed (option A).** The new coil's outer pad (LA) is at (120.0, 151.53) and its inner
pad (LB) at (114.4, 147.33), KiCad mm. The NTUAS LA via at (120.0, 150.7) would sit inside coil
turn 2 (same net, so DRC is silent, but it bypasses part of the coil), so it is gone: LA leaves
U1 as before, runs down B.Cu at x = 115.8 to y = 152.9 (outside the coil), across to a via at
(120.0, 152.9) and back up F.Cu to the pad. LB drops from (114.7, 146.58) to its pad at
(114.4, 147.33). The builder rebuilds the whole LA/LB set each run. The coil band is wider now
(2.0–7.1 mm from the edge, was ~3.8–6.5 on NTUAS). Widening the gaps to 0.50 mm (2026-10-27)
moved the inner edge from 6.5 to 7.1 mm, but no LED, bus or silk moved: the closest other-net
F.Cu copper is the TP1 via at (176.25, 107.8), about 0.4 mm away. TP1–TP3 themselves are on
B.Cu, under the inner turn.

## Manufacturing notes

Same as the NTUAS card: black
mask, ENIG, top-side assembly, but **0.8 mm** thick (NTUAS: 1.0 mm); set PCB Thickness to 0.8
in the JLC quote. **36 fitted parts** (the NTUAS 31 plus D21, Q1, R7, R8,
C4); C1 (not fitted), L1 (copper) and TP1–TP3 (bare pads on the back) are not assembled. U2 is
the VQFN-20 ATtiny1616-MNR (LCSC C507118) at 90° here. `scripts/export-card.py` corrects U2
(-90°) and Q1 (180°) for JLC's library, as checked on the Terminal card in JLCONE on 2026-10-28.
Orbit's own placement preview was checked the same day (see Ordering below).

Physical validation (NFC range, LED brightness, harvested power) is still unproven on the NTUAS
card and inherited here.

## Ordering (JLCPCB)

The first batch (Y8, 2026-10-28) was 20 PCBs with 15 assembled, about US$141 before shipping.

- **PCB:** 0.8 mm FR-4, black mask, ENIG, 1 oz, tented 0.3 mm vias, order number removed,
  Confirm Production File on.
- **Assembly:** Standard PCBA, top side, JLC adds and removes the edge rails, Confirm Parts
  Placement on. Upload the `bom.csv` and `cpl.csv` from `scripts/export-card.py`, which already
  apply the U2 and Q1 rotation offsets.
- **Placement preview:** every LED needs JLC's "−" on pad 1 (the cathode); U1, U2 and Q1 need
  their pin-1 dots on pad 1. D21 is bidirectional.
- Y8 had no LED polarity marks (see "LED cathode dots"), so JLC's engineer revised the LED
  placement and asked for a polarity check. The current boards have the marks.
- Approve the placement within 72 h (JLC auto-confirms after that), then flash the firmware
  over TP1–TP3.

## Bring-up (2026-11-08)

Full log: `docs/bringup/2026-11-08-orbit-first-cards.md`.

- **R6 is too high for the UPDI Friend.** With R6 = 4.7 kΩ the chip's reply never pulls the
  Friend's line (1 kΩ pull-up) below about 2.7 V, so avrdude fails at link initialization. Bridge
  R6 to program; it can stay bridged (D21 still clamps TP1). **Fixed in the design
  (2026-11-08): R6 is now 0 Ω** (UNI-ROYAL 0402WGF0000TCE, LCSC C17168, JLC basic) in the
  schematic, the board and `fab/reference/card-netlist.xml`. No resistor is needed on the card: the UPDI Friend already has 1 kΩ in series with its TX, it powers the card from the same 3 V supply, and D21 clamps static on TP1. Every setup known to work has none (R6 bridged on Y8, Wilson Harper's card).
  470 Ω (SpenceKonde's value for programmers that drive UPDI directly) was tried first
  and dropped untested: a divider estimate put the low level near 1.1 V against the
  Friend's 1 kΩ. The 0402 pads stay, so a resistor can be fitted for another programmer.
  Only future orders get it: the 15 assembled Y8 cards have 4.7 kΩ and still
  need R6 bridged.
- On card 2 with R6 bridged: signature `0x1e9421`, fuses set, all 20 LEDs and the Orbit
  animation verified on 3.3 V programmer power. The tag still reads with JP1 fitted.
- Harvested power starts the MCU and passes the VLM check with the card on the back of an
  iPhone, without C1. The iPhone field lasts only about 30–65 ms per tap, so the animation
  (100 ms qualification, 800 ms fade-in) does not show there; firmware now flashes D16 at
  power-up so each iPhone poll gives a blink. On Android the full animation runs.
- Card 1 was reverse-powered several times by a cable whose colours differ from Adafruit's
  guide, and has not been rechecked.

## Pre-order checks (2026-10-26)

Estimates and design-file checks only. None replaces tapping a phone on an assembled card.

**Antenna (rev B: option A).** L1 is now `nfc-card:NFC_Antenna_Rect_ID1_4turn_W090`: 4 turns
at a 2.0 mm inset, 0.90 mm track, **0.50 mm gap** (1.40 mm pitch). FastHenry
(`sim/fasthenry_coil.py`, skin effect resolved) gives **2.37 µH at 13.56 MHz**, so 14.63 MHz at
the NTAG's 50 pF (13.82–15.59 MHz over 44–56 pF), aiming high so C1 (not fitted) can tune it
down after a NanoVNA check: 14.41 / 14.17 / 13.87 MHz with C1 = 1.5 / 3.3 / 5.6 pF. Area ×
turns is 138 cm². Until 2026-10-27 the gap was 0.30 mm on AN11276's 2.39 µH, but FastHenry gives
that coil 2.49 µH: 14.26 MHz at 50 pF and 13.48 MHz at 56 pF, under the carrier with no fix,
since C1 only lowers it. `sim/coil_inductance.py` (Neumann integrals over the routed copper)
agrees, 2.388 µH at low frequency against FastHenry's 2.368 µH at 13.56 MHz. Board parasitics are
not in either number and will pull the frequency down a little. The NTUAS coil's estimate below is kept for reference:
Mohan et al. (IEEE JSSC 34(10), 1999), 77.6 × 45.98 mm outer, 4 turns, 0.35/0.35 mm: modified
Wheeler 2.41 µH → 14.49 MHz, current sheet 2.84 µH → 13.35 MHz, which could sit under 13.56 MHz.

**Copper inside the coil.** `scripts/check_loops.py` (run by `check.sh`) finds no closed copper
loop in any of the 19 routed nets on rev B. The supply loops close through capacitors, which are
near-shorts at 13.56 MHz. The always-closed C2 → JP1 → C3 loop encloses 30.6 mm² (rev A:
36.7 mm²), about 0.9 % of a mean rev B coil turn (~3,500 mm²). The largest supply loop is
C4 → Q1 → JP1 → U1 at 45.6 mm², closed once the firmware turns Q1 on. The gate fails any
supply loop over 100 mm². Lit-LED drive loops reach 102.8 mm² (D3), but an off LED is open at
RF and a lit one has about 400 Ω in series, so they are reported, not gated. The upper B.Cu bus
is 1.6 mm inside the innermost turn at its closest.

**Harvest power budget, LTspice** (`sim/harvest*.cir`). NTAG VOUT is modelled as a current source
clamped near 3.3 V (NXP datasheet Vout,max). C2 + C3 = 200 nF. The MCU is a 0.6 mA load, which is
pessimistic: the datasheet gives 8 MHz active at 3 V as 2.2 mA typical, 3.5 mA max, so 1 MHz scales
to about 0.3–0.45 mA. Each GPIO is 100 Ω (assumed; Microchip publishes only curves). The scan pulse
is the firmware's 62 µs per 250 µs slot. The LED model is fitted to KT-0603R at 2.0 V (typ) and
2.4 V (max) at 20 mA. Minimum VCC with at most two LEDs lit, as this animation runs:

| Harvested current | 0.3 mA | 0.5 mA | 0.75 mA | 1 mA | 2 mA | 5 mA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VCC min, typical LED | 1.00 V | 1.61 V | 2.06 V | 2.41 V | 2.66 V | 3.21 V |
| VCC min, 2.4 V LED | 1.00 V | 1.66 V | 2.25 V | 2.61 V | 2.86 V | 3.24 V |
| LED peak current, typical | – | 0.2 mA | 2.0 mA | 3.5 mA | 3.7 mA | 3.8 mA |

Rev B adds the soft-start reservoir (C4 10 µF behind R7 2.2 kΩ, bypassed by Q1 once the
firmware latches it), which these 200 nF runs do not model; see `sim/vout_reservoir.cir` at the
repo root for its effect on ripple, hold-up and startup.

BODLEVEL0 is 1.7–2.0 V, so the card needs about **0.75–1 mA** from the phone to run. NXP's
typical figure is 5 mA at 2 V; the MCU, not the LEDs, dominates the load. Even with every slot
lit (`harvest.cir`), 1.5 mA holds VCC at or above 2.5 V.

**Brightness is the weaker margin.** The fixed scan gave each LED one 250 µs slot per 5 ms frame
at 62 µs on (1.25 % duty), about 20–46 µA from 2–3.8 mA peaks. Orbit and Terminal now use shared
slots (`firmware/docs/timing.md`): the lit LEDs share 6 slots, so one lit LED is on 7.4 % of the
time (about 150–280 µA) and two are on 3.7 % each (about 75–140 µA). Six lit slots is well under
the every-slot-lit case above. Whether that reads as clearly lit can only be judged on a real card.

**Gerbers (rev A export; redo for rev B).** The zip re-read: 9 layers plus a job file, 1.0 mm, 2 layers, 85.65 × 54.03 mm
including the outline stroke. The drill file has 69 × 0.3 mm holes, matching the 69 vias. B.Mask
and B.Paste are empty (single-sided). Rev B has 76 vias and opens B.Mask for the three test
pads, so these counts change at the next export. `scripts/jlc_dfm.py` (run before saving) widens all
silkscreen, library outlines included, to 0.16 mm, cuts it back 0.25 mm from every pad and hole,
gives U1/U2/Q1 a 0.02 mm mask margin and every via a 0.65 mm pad. The UPDI run over R6 moved up
0.2 mm (concept y 19.4) to clear R6's mask opening. JLCDFM (2026-10-28) then reports no danger or
warning items on the PCB; the SMT notes are as on Terminal (see its README).
